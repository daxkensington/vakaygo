The September draft instead listed stays3/9, tours5/10, dining0/0, events3/5,
transfers4/8, transport5/8 and guides/VIP/spa5/10. Those old terms remain archived.

Promotions reduce the platform share first. A discount greater than the commission
also reduces operator earnings. Example:100 subtotal /20 discount on a tour gives
80 charged,80 operator earnings,0 platform fee, in the same booking currency.
This proposal discloses that behavior; it does not change promotion economics.

Card collection is separate from settlement. Connected-account charges use the
verified Stripe route; platform collection requires a verified direct-settlement
profile. PayPal/Wise/wire are possible operator settlement routes, not traveler
checkout methods. Release and payout jobs record status/ledger entries, not a bank
transfer. Direct settlement still needs human confirmation and a recorded reference.
No guaranteed arrival date, automatic transfer, country coverage, payout currency,
FX rate, external fee or minimum is inferred from a profile field or a weekly job.
The current profile minimum field and cron threshold are not aligned; confirm the
operational arrangement before enabling a route rather than promising automation.
Full and partial refunds retain current code behavior; a partial refund need not
reverse an application fee. Traveler cancellation policy snapshots are unchanged.

The new receipt table follows the listing lifecycle: deletion cascades with listing
deletion, and trusted DB staff can alter it. This is not a legally approved retention
policy or cryptographic consent proof. It is exact application/database audit evidence.

IMPLEMENTATION PREPARED
- Fixed version/fee manifest; parity tests prevent silent contract/pricing drift.
- Current terms plus a clearly labeled verbatim September6 archive; public access is
  limited to those exact paths. Unknown nested routes remain protected.
- Checkbox consent is tied to the displayed version, including refresh/save races.
- App and SQL require the exact new version. Canonical activation checks/locks both
  payment routes; app fallbacks cannot bypass it, including a stale database policy.
- Migration0018 snapshots previous acceptance exactly and records later new consent
  separately. It does not relabel existing signatures or rewrite financial records.
- Reused checkout URLs get a final SQL/eligibility/calendar check after provider reads.
  Revocation closes the exact link; failed expiry is reported for retry, not success.
- Payout UI uses stored checkout earnings; missing historical earnings and mixed or
  unknown currency totals require review. CSV rows retain their recorded currencies.
